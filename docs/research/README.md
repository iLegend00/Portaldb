# PortalDB Research Notes

Research files collect useful evidence and working context before it is ready for the live data model. They may contain official facts, community-wiki facts, player observations, source conflicts, measurements, unresolved mechanics, and planning assumptions.

Research notes are **not automatically production-ready**. Publishing still requires compliance with the [Data Integrity Standard](../standards/data-integrity.md) and [Provenance UX Standard](../standards/provenance-ux.md).

## Internal Research Labels

Use explicit prose labels in research documents:

- **Confirmed fact:** supported by an identified official source.
- **Community-supported fact:** supported by a community reference but not yet promoted as official evidence.
- **Player-observed fact:** directly observed by a player in the supplied context.
- **Approximate measurement:** an estimate whose geometry or precision is not authoritative.
- **Unresolved question:** not yet established well enough to publish as fact.
- **Source conflict:** credible sources currently disagree or describe different conditions.
- **Planning preference:** a personal goal or evaluation choice, not a game-wide rule.

These labels are an internal writing convention only. They do not add or change any visible PortalDB provenance classification.
