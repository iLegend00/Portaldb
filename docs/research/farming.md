# Farming / Homestead Research

**Research status:** Mixed community references, approximate player measurements, observations, and unresolved questions. These notes are not production data.

## Plot Geometry

**Approximate player measurement:** The farm plot appears to be roughly 36 × 36 main grid squares. Each main grid square visually subdivides into roughly 4 × 4 smaller placement squares.

These are not official dimensions. Visible geometry can protrude slightly or fail to align perfectly with the grid.

## Approximate Structure Footprints

## Greenhouse

**Community-wiki facts:**

- Structure decoration and walk-in growhouse with bundled irrigation.
- Soil inside can grow crops in any season.
- Base maximum: one per plot.
- The wiki claims a maximum of two while wearing the Overclocked title.
- Crafted at the Crafting Bench; sell value 250 Tria.
- Collected recipe: Stone Tile 8×4×1 ×8; Wooden Door ×1; Pipe ×20; Glass Pane ×40; Wood ×80.

### Source Conflict

- The community wiki attributes a second Greenhouse to wearing the Overclocked title.
- Existing PortalDB official patch data says the Season 1 Premium Pass grants one additional permanent Greenhouse slot.

These claims are not silently reconciled because they may describe different rules or conditions.

### Practical Placement

**Approximate player planning guidance:** Reserve roughly 0.5–1.0 main-grid square behind a Greenhouse for pipe/service clearance. Pipes may be able to route sideways from behind the structure for placement nearer a wall. This is not an exact placement mechanic.

## Decoration / Buildable Categories

**Community-wiki reference:** Current categories include Structures, Furniture, Mechanical, Hydraulics, and Farm.

Farming-related examples:

- Hydraulics: Pipe, Pipe Corner, Pipe T-Junction, Pump, Sprinkler, Valve, Water Tank.
- Mechanical: Gearbox, Handcrank, Large Cog, Sail, Shaft, Small Cog.
- Farm: Animal Plot.
- Structures: Greenhouse and construction pieces such as tiles, planks, beams, roofs, doors, fences, and windows.

## Build Limit

**Current community/player-reported working assumption:** A plot may allow 500 placed items.

**Player observation:** Each placed object appears to consume one build slot, and Soil Plots appear to count individually.

Unresolved:

- Exact official verification of the 500-item limit.
- Whether a completed Greenhouse counts as one slot.
- Whether an Animal Plot counts as one slot.
- Whether planted crops count separately.
- How special or composite crafted structures are counted.

## Vertical Building

**Current observation:** No vertical limit has been established. Multi-story builds appear conceptually possible, but the exact height cap remains unresolved; no numeric height should be published.

## Farm Layout Planning

Durable planning direction, not game mechanics:

- Cozy, stylized fantasy-farm aesthetic.
- Asymmetrical, natural zoning rather than rigid quadrants.
- Two Greenhouses planned.
- A barn likely containing one or two Animal Plots.
- Custom home, barn, and cosmetic crafting/work area.
- Winding paths and natural zoning.
- Item-count efficiency matters because the 500-object cap remains a working assumption.
- Prefer larger structural pieces when they achieve the same result with fewer placed objects.
