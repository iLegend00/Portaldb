ABOUT THE GAME

What is The Portal?

The Portal [MMORPG] is a Roblox MMORPG from The Box of Trolls Studio. Its official release messaging emphasizes deliberate difficulty and slower progression rather than instant power. The documented game currently spans story quests, combat, jobs/classes, gathering, farming, equipment progression, NPC services, world bosses and other interconnected systems.

Progression: questing, equipment, skills, jobs and stat allocation.
World systems: gathering, farming, shops, crafting/forging services and locations.
Combat: four known jobs — Warrior, Defender, Enchanter and Cleric — with distinct strengths and weaknesses.
